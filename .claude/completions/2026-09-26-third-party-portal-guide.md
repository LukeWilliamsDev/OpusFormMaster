# Third-party portal guide

Created `docs/third-party-portal-user-guide.html` with:

- Opus Form branding and Luke Williams — IT document attribution.
- Plain-language instructions for home, staff, assigned sites, attachments, notes, photos, and help.
- Responsive layout for desktop, tablet, and mobile viewports.
- Print styles suitable for PDF export.
- Existing desktop and mobile third-party portal mockup screenshots.
- Explicit labelling that all example data is fictional/test data.

Supporting screenshots and logo are in `docs/assets/third-party-portal/`.

## Review update

The guide was critiqued against the available portal implementation and mockups, then improved with:

- A five-minute quick-start route.
- Clear separation between fictional screenshots and live records.
- Less misleading upload and approval wording.
- A status glossary and pending-review explanation.
- Mobile fixed-navigation guidance and full-size screenshot links.
- Keyboard focus styles, richer image descriptions, table caption/column scopes, and clearer support information.

An improved PDF was generated at `docs/third-party-portal-user-guide.pdf` and sent to `lukewilliams141@gmail.com` on 26 September 2026.
